#**precious**# 
##by Kristin Takunna##

My body said I should berak...

but I'm just not a fan of berak.

It's just not my hobby,

so I waited...

'till I'm kebelet.

But when I'm done beraking,

I feel so light, 😩

so alive.

All my problems just disappear...

or so I thought.

But I'm sorry, berak...

I still couldn't love you.

You lied.

My project assignments

are still unfinished. 😫

You made me live

in an illusion

for just a minute...😔

A precious minute.

Then reality came back.

My deadlines are still here.

My responsibilities are still here.

And my problems?

They're literally waiting for me

outside the toilet.

I'd rather you leave me alone...

until next time... 💔
